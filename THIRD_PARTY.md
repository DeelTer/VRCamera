# Third party

## Vivecraft

The model and the texture of the camera are those of [Vivecraft](https://github.com/Vivecraft/VivecraftMod), which
are under the GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0. The mod has a copy of them, to show cameras without Vivecraft
installed:

- `src/client/resources/assets/vrcamera/items/camera.json`
- `src/client/resources/assets/vrcamera/models/item/camera.json`
- `src/client/resources/assets/vrcamera/textures/item/camera.png`

Changed: the names of the textures the model refers to, which are in the namespace of this mod.
